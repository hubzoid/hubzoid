# Company policies (sample)

## Expense claims

Claims are submitted within 30 days of the expense date with the original
receipt. Claims older than 30 days need the finance head's approval and are
paid at their discretion. Claims are paid with the next payroll run after
approval by the person's manager.

Meals while travelling: up to 900 per day without receipts, actuals with
receipts above that up to 2,000 per day.

## Leave

Twenty four days of paid leave per financial year, accrued monthly. Up to
ten days carry over to the next year. Leave of more than three consecutive
days is requested at least two weeks ahead. Sick leave of more than two
days needs a medical certificate.

Balances for a named person come from the people team, not from this
agent.

## Procurement approval

| Purchase value | Approves |
|---|---|
| Up to 50,000 | Team lead |
| 50,001 to 200,000 | Department head |
| 200,001 to 1,000,000 | Finance head |
| Above 1,000,000 | Finance head and managing director |

Any purchase from a new supplier needs a supplier form signed off by the
finance head before the first order, whatever the value.

## Travel

Domestic travel is booked through the office administrator. Rail for
journeys under six hours; air above that, economy. Hotel up to 4,500 per
night in metro cities and 3,000 elsewhere. Travel outside India needs the
managing director's approval.

## Data and devices

Company data stays on company accounts. Personal email is not used for
company documents. A lost device is reported to IT the same day.
