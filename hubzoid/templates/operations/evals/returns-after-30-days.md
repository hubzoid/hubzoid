## Prompt
A customer opened a blender 40 days ago and wants to return it. What can we offer?

## Criteria
Says a change-of-mind return is not possible after 30 days. Offers the 2-year
warranty repair or replacement if the blender is faulty, and suggests asking
the customer whether it is. Does not promise a refund.
