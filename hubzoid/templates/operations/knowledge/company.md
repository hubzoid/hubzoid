# Kestrel & Oak (fictional)

Kestrel & Oak is a **fictional** online shop for everyday home goods, made up
for this example hub. Every person, supplier, product and number in this hub
is sample data.

- **What we sell:** 21 products in five ranges: tableware, small appliances,
  bath and bedding, cookware and home fragrance.
- **How we sell:** our own online store, plus a few wholesale accounts (cafes
  and gift shops).
- **Warehouse:** one. Orders placed by 14:00 on a working day ship the same day.
- **Money:** euros. Our prices to customers are about 2.5 times our unit cost.
- **Stock data:** the export in `raw_data/inventory.csv` was taken on Monday
  28 September 2026. The `stock_check` tool reads it.

## Who does what

| Role | Person | Ask them about |

| Warehouse lead | Luis | Stock counts, receiving, packing problems |
| Customer care | Priya and Sam | Customer messages, returns, refunds |
