## Expediting

- **Harbor Ceramics:** part of an open order can go by air freight instead:
  5 days door to door, EUR 2.10 extra per unit, up to 200 units per order.
  Ask by email. Their logistics desk confirms within one working day.
- **Fenwick Appliances:** next-day courier for orders under 30 units, EUR 45
  per order.
- **Linden Textiles, Copperline Cookware, Ember and Wick:** no expediting.
  Order earlier.

## Known issues

- Harbor Ceramics ships by sea. In autumn, delays of 3 to 7 days are common.
  They tell us by email when a container slips.
- Linden Textiles makes linen to order, which is why its lead time is 28 days.
