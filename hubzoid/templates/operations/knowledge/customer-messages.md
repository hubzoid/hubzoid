# Writing to customers

- Plain and warm. Short sentences. Use the customer's first name.
- Say what happened, what we will do, and when. One apology, no more.
- Give a date, not "soon". If we do not know yet, say when we will write again.
- Offer a choice when there is one: wait, switch to a similar product, or
  cancel with a full refund.
- Never promise what the returns policy does not allow.
- Sign as "Kestrel & Oak customer care".

## Example: a delayed order

> Hi [first name],
>
> Your [product] is taking longer than we planned: our supplier's shipment is
> running late. We now expect to send it on [date]. If you would rather not
> wait, reply and we will switch it to [similar product in stock], or cancel
> and refund you in full.
>
> Sorry for the wait.
>
> Kestrel & Oak customer care
