# Returns and refunds

## Change of mind

- Any unused item in its original packaging can be returned within 30 days of
  delivery for a full refund.
- An opened small appliance can be returned within 30 days of delivery if it is
  clean and complete. We refund the price less a EUR 10 handling charge.
- The customer pays return shipping (EUR 5.95, taken from the refund) unless
  the item is faulty.
- After 30 days we do not accept change-of-mind returns.

## Faulty items

- **Within 30 days of delivery:** refund or replacement, the customer chooses.
  We pay return shipping.
- **After 30 days, small appliances:** a 2-year warranty. We arrange a repair
  or a replacement through the supplier, usually within 10 working days. No
  refund.
- **After 30 days, other ranges:** 1 year against manufacturing faults.
  Replacement only.

## Goodwill

- Customer care may offer store credit up to EUR 15 for a delay or a mistake
  on our side. More than that needs the operations lead.

## Refund timing

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the
  return reaching the warehouse.

## Never

- Refund outside this policy without the operations lead's approval.
- Ask a customer to send back a faulty item before sending them a free return
  label.
