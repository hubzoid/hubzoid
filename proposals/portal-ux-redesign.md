# Agent-first administration

User-approved direction: keep existing authorization and execution services; replace the backend-shaped portal with an agent-first experience using Ant Design. Begin with agent access, staged capability edits and review before Save, URL navigation and explicit account/access states. Preserve role checks, inherited permissions and multi-hub isolation. Follow with People details, readable Activity and workflow observability. Track acceptance and remaining work in PORTAL-UX-HANDOVER.md.
